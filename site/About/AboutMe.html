Engineer by profession, Educator by passion

Hi, I'm Vaisakh.

I build multi-domain physical system simulations from first principles, contribute to open-source modeling standards, and break down complex engineering topics into practical tutorials.

Whether I’m modeling transient thermal-fluid networks in Modelica, coupling multi-FMU co-simulations, or scripting local-first AI tools in Python, I care deeply about transparent, deterministic engineering over black-box shortcuts.

Outside of day-to-day engineering work, I run Simulation Workshop—an educational channel and documentation hub dedicated to helping engineers master multi-domain system modeling—and actively contribute to the Modelica Standard Library ecosystem.

⚙️ System Modeling

Thermal-fluid networks, 1D finite volume heat transfer, magnetic circuits, and SSP/FMU co-simulation workflows.

🎥 Simulation Workshop

Creating deep-dive video walkthroughs and open documentation that bridge theoretical equations with live OpenModelica code.

🛠️ Open-Source & Local AI

Contributing to Modelica libraries and building lightweight Python pipelines and local LLM utilities on Linux.
