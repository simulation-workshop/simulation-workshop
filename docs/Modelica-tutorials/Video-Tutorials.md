# Video Tutorials on Modelica

## Introduction to Modelica Language
It is tempting to get started with a new tool by writting something and see the results. But I feel it is important to acknoledge the history 
and the milestones of its development. This video covers the why and how of Modelica's evolution, the concept of modeling physical systems through
equations and an introduction to Open Modelica ecosystem.
{% embed "https://youtu.be/YqpG6gp82uQ?si=Hht30f_BZ3y-nHjZ" title="Me at the zoo" caption="Introduction to Modelica" %}

## Building your first model from scratch - Modelica Basics

Transient heating and cooling of a metal ball can be simulated by incorporating the relationship between heat and temperature along with 
Newton's law of cooling. This is an introductory course in Modelica where we will build our own simulation model using equations.
{% embed "https://youtu.be/KDQhYO-GokA?si=PaiVwkj3nLzf4c11" title="Me at the zoo1" caption="Building your first model" %}

## Modelica Standard Library - The power of pre-validated models
Revisiting the previous problem statement of heating and cooling a metal ball, but this time we will use the components from Modelica
standard library to generate the exact same results as before. We understand the purpose of using library components instead of building
everything from scratch.
{% embed "https://youtu.be/B_ia0dflxCQ?si=ZezLwLH8sNHpefPX" title="Me at the zoo1" caption="Modelica Standard Library" %}

## Building Your own models and connecting them to to MSL Components
Both approaches of building components from scratch and using Modelica standard library has its on pros and cons. So why not get the 
best of both worlds buy building our own components that can interact with other components from the standard library.
We build our custom ball model and an air stream component and make them interact with each other.
{% embed "https://youtu.be/dP30kI8YRTw?si=MN0QkbiIrVK3nVgb" title="Me at the zoo1" caption="Custom components" %}

## Building an Electrical Kettle in Modelica - Introudction to multi-domain simulations
Most of the engineered products are a combination of multiple physical domains and their interactions. We model an electrical kettle
incorporating the electrical, Thermal and control aspects of it. We understand the process of iterative modeling.
{% embed "https://youtu.be/lda62EodEqQ?si=5OaENEFULfQ3ZT_D" title="Me at the zoo1" caption="Multi-domain modeling" %}

## Fluid Property Models in Modelica - Thermodynamics in Simulations
Fluid Flow forms a foundational part in modeling of engineering systems. Thanks to Modelica media library, we have an extensive list of 
media packages under the hood. We will learn what are fluid property models, how to use them and eventually perform a thermodynamic analysis of a power plant using it.
{% embed "https://youtu.be/5SwIYZRZnxo?si=2kdo-AQOpQafoERG" title="Me at the zoo" caption="Fluid Property models" %}
